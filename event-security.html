HIGH-CAPACITY EVENT ARCHITECTURE

Flawless Logistics Behind Every Event

Large public and corporate events bring heightened reputational and physical stakes. Our Event Security Division designs the entire threat suppression plan from venue perimeter ingress and ticketing verification to VIP stage cordons and emergency evacuation corridors.

We interface seamlessly with local authorities (Kenya Police Service, Red Cross, and local county administration) ensuring your event runs without interruption or regulatory friction.

Walk-Through Metal Detector Arches

Rapid-throughput screening lanes equipped with calibrated metal detector gates and hand-held wands for non-intrusive patron inspection.

VIP Holding & Stage Cordon Units

Dedicated, physically imposing personnel stationed at stage wings, backstage green rooms, and executive holding suites.

Emergency Egress & Crush Prevention

Strategic crowd density monitoring to prevent bottlenecking, panic surges, and ensure all emergency fire exits remain completely unimpeded.

Parking Traffic & Valet Security

Structured vehicular marshalling, illumination of perimeter car lots, and protection against vehicle tampering or opportunist theft.
