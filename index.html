CATEGORY 05 // INDUSTRY SECTOR PLAYBOOKS

Safeguarding Kenya's Vital Sectors

Specialized security playbooks engineered to address the distinct threat vectors of each corporate and residential vertical.

Commercial Real Estate

High-rise corporate towers and office parks require seamless, professional visitor screening, subterranean parking protection, and executive reception etiquette.

Residential Communities

Gated estates and luxury apartments benefit from perimeter barrier defenses, automated resident tag passes, night canine patrols, and rapid panic button dispatch.

Supermarkets & Retail Hubs

Minimizing inventory shrinkage and internal fraud through alert uniformed deterrence at checkouts and discreet plainclothes loss prevention personnel.

High-Net-Worth Individuals

Dedicated personal protection details, secure armored chauffeur transit, advance route reconnaissance, and discrete residential security details.
